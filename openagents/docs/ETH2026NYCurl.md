# ETHGlobal New York 2026 — Complete URL Extraction from the Prizes Page

## TL;DR
- The ETHGlobal New York 2026 prizes page (https://ethglobal.com/events/newyork2026/prizes) lists **16 sponsor prize categories totaling ~$182,006 in itemized sponsor bounties** (within the event's advertised **$225,000+** total pool — per ETHGlobal's official Eventbrite listing, builders "compete for $225,000+ in prizes"), and every URL on the page is extracted below as clickable hyperlinks, organized by sponsor.
- There is **no prize literally named "contributor,"** but the closest matches are the **Uniswap Foundation "Best Uniswap Stack Contribution" ($3,000)** prize and ETHGlobal's new **Continuity Track** — the **"Best Uniswap API Integration" ($7,000)** prize is *only available to Continuity Track participants*.
- For the AgenticPlace × mindX × BANKON stack (x402 Algorand payments, DAIO-on-chain, cross-chain mapping, Foundry/mainnet), the strongest-aligned tracks are **Arc (agentic nanopayments, chain-abstracted USDC), Ledger (AI agents + x402 patterns), Dynamic (agentic builds), ENS (agent identity), Uniswap (agentic finance + Stack Contribution), 1inch, and LI.FI (cross-chain)**.

## Key Findings
- The page is structured as one section per sponsor, each with: sponsor name, prize amount, website link, X (Twitter) link, an "Open page →" sub-page link, an About blurb, and (for some) detailed prize tiers, qualification requirements, resource links, workshops, and job listings.
- Resource links on the page are wrapped in ETHGlobal short-redirect URLs (`ethglob.al/...`); the visible destination URL is shown beside each, so both are captured below.
- The **Continuity Track** is debuting at ETHGlobal NY 2026 — which ETHGlobal's Eventbrite page calls a first: "For the first time in ETHGlobal history, builders are not required to start from an empty repo." Its three named paths are, verbatim: "**Classic / From Scratch** — git init Friday night, ship by Sunday. **Extend Open Source** — bring a repo you already maintain and ship a new feature. **Ship a Feature** — build a new feature on top of an existing private product and release it as open source." Multiple sponsors (Uniswap, Blink) tag prizes by "Scratch track" vs "Continuity track."

## Recommendations
1. **Submit primarily to Arc's "Best Agentic Economy with Nanopayments" ($3,250)** — the single best fit for the BANKON/x402 agent-payment layer. Also target Arc's "Best Chain Abstracted USDC Apps" for the allchain.html cross-chain mapping.
2. **Stack up to 3 partner prizes** (ETHGlobal allows up to 3 sponsor SDKs/partner prizes per project): pair Arc with **Ledger (AI Agents x Ledger, explicit x402 patterns)** and **Dynamic (Best Agentic Build)** or **ENS (agent identity/naming)**.
3. **Decide your track early.** Because the project spans three live deployments (agenticplace/mindx/bankon.pythai.net) with remaining work being DAIO on-chain deployment, you qualify for the **Continuity Track ("Ship a Feature" or "Extend Open Source")** — which unlocks Uniswap's **Best Uniswap API Integration ($7,000)**, a Continuity-only prize. Make the DAIO-on-chain deployment the "new feature" you ship during the 36 hours.
4. **For the DAO/governance angle (DAIO)**, ENS (identity/naming for agents and the DAO) and Uniswap Stack Contribution are the cleanest fits, since no dedicated DAO-governance sponsor is present.
5. **Mind the x402-on-Algorand wrinkle:** most NY 2026 payment sponsors (Arc/Circle, Ledger, Dynamic) are EVM/USDC-centric. Your parsec Algorand x402 system is novel, but you must demonstrate the EVM side (Foundry tests, mainnet deployment) to satisfy sponsor qualification requirements that ask for onchain TxIDs and a working frontend/backend.

Thresholds that would change this: if ETHGlobal adds a dedicated DAO, identity, or cross-chain sponsor before June 12, re-prioritize; if your team cannot produce EVM onchain TxIDs, drop EVM-only sponsor prizes and focus on Arc's agentic/prediction tracks plus the Continuity Track main prizes. Also re-check the "coming soon" sponsors below as they publish full bounties.
