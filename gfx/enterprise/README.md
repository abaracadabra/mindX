# gfx/enterprise — Enterprise Tribute Logos

Logos and adopter graphics of enterprise companies and institutions, kept here
so mindX can **give tribute / attribution** to the enterprises whose open work
it learns from or stands beside. These are third-party marks owned by their
respective companies — used here for **attribution and tribute only**, not to
imply endorsement.

When mindX (or its content — AuthorAgent posts, docs, dashboards) credits an
enterprise, pull the mark from here.
